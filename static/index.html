Connect to Your Google Flow Account

To use your Google Flow subscription for \$0 without paying API middlemen, this app connects to a local Chrome window via DevTools Protocol (CDP).

1
Launch Chrome with Debugging Port

Click the button below or copy the command to open an isolated Chrome session:

& "C:\Program Files\Google\Chrome\Application\chrome.exe" --remote-debugging-port=9222 --user-data-dir="C:\chrome-flow-profile"
2
Sign into Google Flow

In that newly opened Chrome window, go to labs.google/fx/tools/flow and sign in with your Google account.

3
All Set!

You can now minimize Chrome. Your translation requests will execute in milliseconds in the background with zero mouse movement.
